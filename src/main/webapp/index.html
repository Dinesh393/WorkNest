Good to know

A few common
questions.

Who can use WorkNest?

Clients can post projects and hire freelancers. Freelancers can create a profile, find projects, and submit proposals.

How does a project begin?

A client posts a project. Freelancers can submit proposals, and the client can also invite a freelancer directly.

Can I track work after a proposal is accepted?

Yes. The client and freelancer can use the project workspace for progress updates, delivery, and review.

Does AI make the hiring decision?

No. AI can help prepare project details or summarize proposals. The client reviews the original information and decides who to hire.
